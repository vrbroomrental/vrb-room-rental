FAQs

Message us
Is a room available right now?

Usually not — the building is normally full, and rooms open only as tenants move out. They are let on a first-come basis. Most people message us or ring +63 915 211 7123 to be told as soon as one is released, which is the surest way to get it.

What are the requirements?

One valid ID. We do not ask for an employment certificate, a barangay clearance or a co-maker — bring your ID and the move-in payment and you can sign.

Do you accept short stays?

Yes, where a room is vacant. The minimum is one week, billed at a daily rate. The twelve-month lease remains our standard arrangement, so short stays depend on what is free at the time — inquire to check.

Can I cook in my room?

No — there is no kitchen, private or shared, and cooking in rooms is not allowed. There are carinderias and eateries within the block.

What is billed on top of rent?

Electricity is metered per room and billed on your consumption. Water is billed per person, per day. Garbage collection and janitorial service are charged separately, and Wi-Fi is optional. Your rent covers the room itself.

Is there a bathroom in the room?

No. Each floor has one maintained shared bathroom.

Can I pay the deposit in installments?

Often, yes — subject to approval. Raise it when you inquire and we will talk it through.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes. We are pet-friendly, with house rules so the arrangement stays fair to everyone on your floor.

Are you a registered business?

Yes — DTI-registered, with a current business permit, and BIR-registered as Non-VAT. The DTI and permit numbers are listed above if you would like to check them.
