Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated 3 August 2026

Who We Are

VRB Room Rental is a family-owned and operated residential rental business located at 1178 Julio Nakpil Street, Barangay 686, Paco, Manila 1007, and is registered with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) under Registration No. 5606779.

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), VRB Room Rental is the Personal Information Controller for the personal information described in this Privacy Policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at info@vrbroomrental.com or +63 915 211 7123.

If You Only Visit Our Website

Your privacy is important to us. No non-essential tracking technologies are activated unless you choose “Accept All” on our cookie notice. If you select “Decline,” only essential website functions will operate.

Your cookie preference is stored locally on your device under vrb-consent and is never transmitted to our servers.

Website Analytics

If you consent, Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use our website by collecting information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Device and browser type

IP addresses are anonymized before processing. This information is used solely to improve our website and user experience.

Embedded Google Maps

The map displayed on our Contact page is provided by Google. If you consent to cookies, Google may set its own cookies and process information according to its own privacy policy, which is separate from ours.

Website Performance Statistics

Cloudflare, which delivers this website, collects anonymous page request statistics. This process does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors.

Short Links

Links beginning with links.vrbroomrental.com record anonymous click counts so we can understand which links and buttons are most useful to visitors.

If You Contact Us About a Room

If you contact us by phone, email, or message, we receive the information you choose to provide, which typically includes:

  • Your name
  • Contact number
  • Rental requirements or inquiry

We use this information to respond to your inquiry and to maintain our waiting list for available rooms.

If you contact us through Facebook Messenger, your messages are also processed by Meta in accordance with Meta’s privacy policy.

If You Become a Tenant

To prepare your lease agreement, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Contact information
  • One valid government-issued identification card

A copy of your identification is attached to your lease agreement, and a digital copy may also remain within your Messenger conversation with us if it was provided there.

We also maintain records relating to rental payments and tenancy.

These records are retained throughout your tenancy and afterwards for as long as required by applicable tax, accounting, and legal obligations. They are used solely for administering your tenancy and complying with those obligations.

CCTV

For the safety and security of residents and visitors, CCTV cameras monitor common areas of the property, including:

  • Building entrance
  • Stairwell
  • Corridors

No cameras are installed inside private rooms or bathrooms.

Footage is generally retained for approximately two weeks before being automatically overwritten.

We do not release CCTV footage upon request. Copies may only be disclosed when required by law, including upon presentation of a barangay endorsement, a formal request from law enforcement, or another legally authorized request.

Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising purposes.

Where necessary to operate our business, certain service providers may process information on our behalf:

  • Meta — Messages and files sent through Facebook Messenger
  • Google — Website analytics and embedded Google Maps (with your consent)
  • Cloudflare and GitHub — Website hosting and delivery
  • Apple iCloud — Business email services
  • Rebrandly — Short links and anonymous click statistics

Outside of these service providers, your information is accessible only to the family members who own and operate VRB Room Rental.

We do not currently employ staff. Should this change in the future, access to personal information will be limited to individuals who require it to perform their duties.

Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your information where we are no longer legally required to retain it
  • Object to certain forms of processing
  • Request a copy of your personal information in a portable format, where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@vrbroomrental.com.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes become effective immediately upon publication on this website. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will be revised to reflect the most recent version.
