Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated 3 August 2026

Who we are

VRB Room Rental is a family-run residence at 1178 Julio Nakpil St., Barangay 686, Paco, Manila 1007, registered with the DTI under No. 5606779. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) we are the personal information controller for the data described here.

Reach us at info@vrbroomrental.com or +63 915 211 7123 about anything on this page.

If you only visit this website

Nothing that identifies you runs until you press Accept all on the cookie notice. Choose Decline and only the essentials load.

  • Your cookie choice is stored on your own device under vrb-consent. It never leaves your browser.
  • Analytics — if you accept, Google Analytics records which pages are read, roughly where visitors are from, and what device they use. IP addresses are anonymised. We use it only to improve the site.
  • The map on our contact page loads from Google if you accept. Google sets its own cookies and applies its own privacy policy, not ours.
  • Page counts are also collected by Cloudflare, which serves this site. This is cookieless and does not identify you.
  • Short links beginning links.vrbroomrental.com count clicks so we know which buttons people use.

If you enquire about a room

When you message, call or email us we receive whatever you choose to send — usually a name, a contact number, and what you are looking for. We use it to answer you and to keep track of who is waiting for a room. Messages sent through Facebook Messenger also pass through Meta, under their privacy policy.

If you become a tenant

To sign a lease we need one valid government ID, along with your name and contact details. A copy is attached to your contract, and a digital copy may be held in our Messenger conversation with you. We also keep a record of payments.

We keep these for as long as you are a tenant, and afterwards for as long as we are required to for tax, accounting and legal purposes. We do not use them for anything other than managing your tenancy and meeting those obligations.

CCTV

Cameras record the common areas — the entrance, stairwell and corridors. There are no cameras inside any room or bathroom.

Footage is kept for roughly two weeks and is then overwritten automatically. We do not hand out copies on request. A copy is released only against a barangay endorsement letter or a formal police request, or where the law otherwise requires it.

Who else sees your information

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising. Certain service providers necessarily process it so the business can run:

  • Meta — messages and any files you send through Messenger
  • Google — website analytics and the embedded map, if you accept
  • Cloudflare and GitHub — serving this website
  • Apple iCloud — our email
  • Rebrandly — the short links and their click counts

Beyond these, information is seen only by the family who own and run the building. We have no staff at present; if that changes, access will be limited to those who need it to do their work.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct anything wrong, or delete it where we are not required to keep it. You may also object to how we use it, or ask for a copy in a portable form.

Write to info@vrbroomrental.com and we will respond. If you are not satisfied, you may bring a complaint to the National Privacy Commission.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be signposted on the site.
